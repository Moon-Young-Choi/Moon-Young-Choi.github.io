00 / Overview

A spread is only a candidate.

The engine treats a cycle as a sequence of executable conversions, not a product of midpoint prices. Each leg consumes the correct side of a synthetic order book, carries fees and partial fills forward, and preserves any asset that cannot complete the cycle.

05 / Safety gates

Live readiness fails closed.

A profitable replay cannot bypass stale data, grouped depth, timing, balance, permission, or policy checks. Null and disabled safety settings are invalid in guarded mode.

    Locked

08 / Boundary

What this evidence does not claim.

Synthetic replay evidence only. No live orders, balances, or profit claims are included.

  • Synthetic tapeYes
  • Live market callsNo
  • Orders placedNo

01

Fail-closed freshness

Guarded execution now requires 1,000 ms book age, 500 ms inter-leg skew and 500 ms observe-to-verify drift limits.

02

Mode-aware profit floor

Observe and simulation keep a 0 bp buffer; guarded execution adds a configurable 10 bp margin.

03

Bounded reconciliation

A 500 ms acknowledgement or 3,000 ms reconciliation breach stops the next leg and records the residual.

04

Portable evidence

A platform-independent test command and deterministic portfolio artifact keep the public record reproducible.
